# 💄 Nykaa Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Overview
The Nykaa Sales Analysis Dashboard provides a comprehensive view of sales performance, customer behavior, and product trends. It helps identify high-performing categories, revenue patterns, and business opportunities through interactive visualizations.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze overall sales performance
- Track revenue and profit
- Identify top-selling product categories
- Understand customer purchasing behavior
- Monitor key business KPIs

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features
- KPI Cards
- Sales Trend Analysis
- Category-wise Sales
- Brand Performance
- Customer Insights
- Interactive Filters & Slicers

---

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Excel



## 📈 Key Insights
- Identified best-performing product categories.
- Tracked sales growth trends.
- Compared revenue across brands.
- Analyzed customer purchasing patterns.
